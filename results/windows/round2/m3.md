# M3 measurements on Windows (round 2, handoff §3; 2026-11-14)

RTX 3060 Laptop (nvidia ampere, 592.00) and Intel UHD (gen-12lp, 31.0.101.5333), Chrome 148 headless, D3D12, on AC,
Turbo plan, nothing else on the GPU. Commands in `test/bench/README.md` (`--skip-validation --power=high|low`);
raw JSON and logs in this folder (`{rtx,intel}-{throughput,throughput-raf,storage-usage,subgroups,f16}.*`).
M5 Pro numbers for comparison: `results/m3/`.

**Caveat:** the RTX heated from 54 to 83 °C over the two throughput runs (about 6 minutes each, back to back),
so its throughput numbers may include clock drops. The later modes ran at 59–78 °C; same-run ratios.

Notes:
- `shuffle` "differ" on the RTX too (9.6 M of 80.7 M values, worst 113 rgba8 steps), not only on Intel's smaller
  subgroups as the brief expected: the prototype's position mapping assumes a lane layout D3D12 doesn't give.
  Timing only.
- f16 without the overflow guard (`f16`) gives non-finite values in 9.9 M of 80.7 M rgba16float texels on both GPUs.
- The throughput mode's per-size rows and the rAF-paced run (no timestamps) are in the logs; the rAF run orders
  the strategies as the queue-paced one.
