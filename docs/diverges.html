Where we diverge from SPD, nvpro and the WebGPU community

Every place this library does something different from AMD FidelityFX SPD, nvpro_pyramid, the WebGPU community ports and the gpuweb #386 / #5253 requests. Each entry gives the reason, the effort to converge, and the quality and performance effect.

Performance labels: measured means Apple M5 Pro runs from SPEC §2.2, §4 and §8. Nothing has been measured on NVIDIA, AMD or Intel yet, so everything else is an estimate or unknown.

Where it stands

Features are built and tested on the Apple M5 Pro. What's left before M3 tuning is measurement on other vendors: the two risk entries (17, 18) and every performance claim depend on it.

Windows run sheet

On the laptop with the RTX 3060 and Intel UHD. --power=high or --power=low picks the GPU; each run prints the adapter it used. Close other GPU apps and plug in power: these numbers become the cross-vendor baseline.

1920×1080, as we batch it today

A batch ends wherever the next 2:1 reduction is inexact. This chain takes 7 dispatches. SPD takes 1 by dropping edges; our oddSizes: 'spd' does the same in 2 and measured 0.66× the time. The exact tail kernel (opt-in) merges the last five levels into one dispatch for 4 in total, with unchanged results, but measured 0.83–1.07×. nvpro would carry odd levels in pairs with a halo (entries 2 and 3).
