downsampler examples

Small pages, one job each. Every page runs on your GPU and checks its own output against a CPU reference: look for the PASS lines in its readout. Start them with npm run examples and open http://localhost:8080/examples/. (WebGPU needs localhost, or HTTPS on another device.)

  • Mip chain
    Every level of a texture from level 0, with a level viewer; 'exact' vs 'spd' odd sizes and the tail kernel.
  • Hi-Z from depth
    A (min, max) pyramid from a depth buffer through a separate source, recorded with encodePass; conservativeness checked.
  • Power-of-two Hi-Z
    A 1920 × 1080 depth buffer into a 1024 × 512 (or 2048 × 1024) pyramid with source.resample: 'area': conservative against every depth texel, next to a 2×2-tap pyramid that misses.
  • Odd sizes
    'exact', 'spd' and 'box' side by side at 31 × 31, 1129 × 635 and 1477 × 831, against the area-weighted ideal, down to the 1×1.
  • Partial updates
    Paint into a texture and update only what depends on the brush with rect, outlined per level; one face of a 6-layer array with layers. Bit-identical to a full recompute.
  • Block statistics (Reducer)
    A load hook and per-channel ops: mean, variance, max and count per 8² and 32² block, in one dispatch.
  • Exposure (ScalarReduction)
    Log-average luminance of an HDR frame in two dispatches, with a finish hook that computes the exposure on the GPU.
  • three.js
    downsampler/three mipmaps a StorageTexture, side by side with three's own render-pass mipmaps. Loads three from a CDN.
